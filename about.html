About OM SAI

Interior design and construction, run the way we'd want it run for our own home.

Company overview

Built on trades, not just design boards

OM SAI Interiors & Construction started as a small painting and plumbing outfit before growing into a full interior design and construction company. We kept the trades in-house on purpose — a designer who understands what a plumber or electrician actually needs draws better plans.

Today we run interior design, painting, ceilings, plumbing, electrical, UPVC and granite work as one coordinated team, so you deal with a single point of contact from first sketch to final invoice.
